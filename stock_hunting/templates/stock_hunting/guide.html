{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Steps 10–11 #}
10 Test (bulk se pehle)

Tareeqa chunein:

  • Dropship par break-even price par bech kar dekhein, ya
  • Thodi quantity (local se) mangwa kar target price par bechein.

Record karein: kitne din, kitni sales, roz kitne order. Roz order aa rahe hain → source karein. Warna chhod dein. (Tool: roz {{ conf.min_orders_per_day|floatformat:"-2" }}+ order = source.)

11 Source karein

Pehle local se, phir China se 200–300 pieces.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- White label notes #}
White label kya hai
  • Unbranded – product par apna logo nahi hota.
  • Inventory (maal) aap ke paas hoti hai, aap eBay par bechte hain.
  • Shuru karna aasan hai.
Maal kahan se (sources)
  • Apne jaan-pehchan ke manufacturers – pehle eBay par sale line aur price range check karein
  • Pak made products
  • Local wholesale markets
  • Online wholesale suppliers (USA / UK / Australia) – har mulk ki apni wholesale market hoti hai
  • China suppliers – zyada quantity, delivery rate kam
UK market (ebay.co.uk)
  • Private account: stock model sab se behtar (dropshipping nahi) – eBay ko fee nahi deni padti.
  • Business account: har sale par 13.25% fee.
Yaad rakhein
  • Price {{ conf.price_min|floatformat:"-2" }} se neeche na jayein – sasti cheez mein dropshippers aa kar market kharab kar dete hain.
  • Research tool par avg sold price, free postage aur total sellers dekhein.
  • Sellers par nazar rakhein (spy) – zyada price + zyada sales wala product chunein.
  • Bulk se pehle hamesha chhota test.
Sourcing se pehle – mazeed achhi baatein

Ye tool ke checks mein shamil nahi, lekin maal mangwane se pehle zaroor sochein:

  • Munafa: landing cost (maal + shipping + custom) bechne ki price ka 35% se kam ho.
  • Halka aur chhota product – warna shipping munafa kha jati hai.
  • Na tootne wala – glass jaisi cheez returns barhati hai.
  • Brand / patent ka masla na ho – bade brand jaisa product eBay par VeRO ka khatra hai.
  • Kam variations (1–2 rang) – zyada size/rang stock phansate hain.
  • Kam returns wali category – electronics aur size wale kapdon mein returns zyada.
