Follow these steps once. After that every product you save here is also written to your Google Sheet, and products you add in the sheet come back here when you sync.

  1. Open your Google Sheet → menu Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Code.gs: delete everything in the editor, then paste the code from Code.gs (download).
    Your sync key is already inside this file.
  3. Form.html: click ＋ next to “Files” → HTML → name it exactly Form, delete what's inside, and paste Form.html.
  4. Save (💾 icon or Ctrl+S).
  5. Click Deploy → New deployment → gear ⚙ → Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone → Deploy. Google asks for permission the first time: Authorize access → choose your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.
  6. Copy the Web app URL (it ends with /exec) and paste it below.

One-click button
eBay · Amazon · AliExpress · Temu · CJ · any shop

Open a product page and click this button in your bookmarks bar – the details go straight into your open Add product form (or a new one opens). No copy-paste.

eBay Tool Drag this green button onto your bookmarks bar.
  1. Bookmarks bar not showing? Press Ctrl+Shift+B. (Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.)
  2. Keep Add product open in one tab.
  3. eBay listing → click eBay Tool: the first one fills the product, the next ones are added as competitors (only the biggest competitor's feedback and sold are kept).
  4. Supplier page (Amazon, AliExpress, Temu, CJ, any shop) → click eBay Tool: supplier link, cost, shipping and the picture are filled.
  5. Check the form and click Submit – nothing is saved without you.
Use the tool at the same address every time (this button was made for {{ request.get_host }}). If something isn't found on a page, that field stays empty with a note – type it yourself. Prices in other currencies (e.g. Rs) are not converted: switch the site to US $ / a US delivery address.
